Welcome

Learn how to protect your passwords, recognise phishing messages and keep your online accounts safe.

🔑 Strong Passwords

A strong password helps protect your personal information and online accounts.

  • Use at least 12 characters.
  • Use a different password for every account.
  • Combine uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers and symbols.
  • Use a password manager if possible.

🎣 Phishing Awareness

Phishing messages try to steal personal information or make users click a dangerous link.

  • Check the sender's email address carefully.
  • Be suspicious of urgent requests.
  • Do not open unexpected links or attachments.
  • Report suspicious messages to the university.

🔐 Account Protection

Extra security measures can reduce the risk of unauthorised access to online accounts.

  • Turn on multi-factor authentication.
  • Keep applications and devices updated.
  • Do not share login details with other people.
  • Review account activity regularly.
